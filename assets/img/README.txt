DROP YOUR IMAGES AND VIDEOS HERE
================================

The site works right now with palette-matched posters. To use your real
photos, just drop files here with these exact names — no code changes needed.

THE 4 HOME-PAGE FILM CHAPTERS (photos)
--------------------------------------
Put these files directly in this folder (assets/img/):

  chapter-01.jpg   The land  — aerial descent onto the bare coastal plot
  chapter-02.jpg   The plan  — wireframe / blueprint rising over the land
  chapter-03.jpg   Becomes real — the finished villa in golden light
  chapter-04.jpg   The handover — the villa / interior with the ocean view

The moment a file is here, it replaces the poster automatically, and the
scroll parallax runs on it. (.png / .webp also work — just keep the name,
e.g. chapter-01.png.)

THE 4 SCROLL VIDEOS (later)
---------------------------
When your videos are ready, drop them in the assets/video/ folder as:

  chapter-01.mp4   (or .webm)
  chapter-02.mp4
  chapter-03.mp4
  chapter-04.mp4

The home page uses the video automatically when it finds one, and falls
back to the photo (then the poster) on phones and reduced-motion.
Tip: for the smoothest scroll-scrub later, encode all-intra (GOP 1) —
see the cinematic-scroll skill's encode-hero.sh.

PROJECT / ARTICLE COVERS
------------------------
In the admin you can either type a filename that lives in this folder,
or upload an image straight from the form (it is saved here for you).
