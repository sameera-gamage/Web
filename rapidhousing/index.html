The studio

We take a building from the first line to the last floor.

RapidHousing is a full-service architecture studio. Architects, engineers, and builders working in one room, so an idea moves from sketch to structure without being handed off, watered down, or slowed to a crawl.

What we do

Three disciplines, one roof.

Most firms hand your project between studios. We keep design, engineering, and delivery on the same team, so nothing gets lost in the gaps.

01

Design

Concept, form, and every drawing in between. We shape spaces people actually want to be in, then draw them to the millimetre.

02

Engineering

Structure, services, and code, solved alongside the design instead of after it. The building stands up because it was engineered from day one.

03

Delivery

On site, on budget, on time. We run the build ourselves, so the thing that gets made is the thing that was drawn.
