MATERIAL
The Tactile Board

Material Curation

Honed Travertine

Book-matched travertine honed to a soft matte finish, cross-cut for consistent veining across large-format slabs on floors and vanity tops.

Climate note — dense, low-porosity stone rated for tropical humidity; resists staining on pool decks and terraces

Board-Formed Concrete

Cast in vertical timber formwork to retain the grain imprint of the boards, left exposed as structure and finish in one pour.

Climate note — sealed with a breathable hydrophobic coating; performs through monsoon cycles without efflorescence

Kiln-Dried Ceylon Teak

Locally sourced and kiln-dried to twelve percent moisture content before joinery, used for screens, decking and cabinetry.

Climate note — natural oil content resists rot and termite activity; ages to a silver-grey patina if left untreated

Matte Aluminum

Powder-coated extruded aluminum framing for screens, louvres and door systems, specified against the coastal salt line.

Climate note — marine-grade alloy rated for coastal salt exposure; will not corrode or warp
